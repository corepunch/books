# App icon

Created with the built-in imagegen editing tool from the supplied book-and-ship
illustration. The generated opaque square master is `app-icon-master.png`
(1254 × 1254); `AppIcon.xcassets` contains its 1024 × 1024 app icon, resized with
macOS sips. `actool` generates the required iPad sizes. Corners are left square for
the system mask.

Prompt:

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: production iPad app icon. Input image 1 is the edit target. Upscale and faithfully refine the provided storybook illustration into a crisp high-resolution square 2048x2048 app icon. Preserve the exact visual identity and arrangement: open cream-paged blue hardcover book in foreground, curling luminous turquoise ocean waves, wooden golden-brown sailing boat with large cream sail and small red pennant centered above book, moon upper left, small golden stars and deep blue night sky. Match original rich blue/gold palette, cheerful painted illustration, soft luminous shading. Adapt the slightly portrait frame to a square by subtly extending background sideways; preserve full book and sail. Clean small-resolution artifacts and sharpen illustrative details without changing design. Fill the entire square edge to edge with opaque artwork; no rounded corners baked in, no border, no text, no badges, no mockup, no new objects. Keep all principal subjects clear inside icon safe area.
