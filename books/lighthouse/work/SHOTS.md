# Shot list — Огонь на маяке

One camera per picture; consecutive pages of one moment share a camera. Cameras live in the
scene named in the second column; `comment` in the scene is the story question and the staging.
Text zones are normalized `x y width height`, listed in reading order with their type scales.
Body scale is 1; the two dramatic openings use 1.65. Headless `text_regions` reports fit for
each block. Tide is the `Sea` group raised in centimetres.

## Colour script

| Pages | Light and mood |
|---|---|
| cottage | warm lamplight inside, violet storm dusk in the window |
| harbour, fork | low gold sun under dark cloud, wind |
| flats, cliffs | the gold fades to copper; water turns slate as the tide rises |
| channel | last light, white horses, the hut stove the only warmth |
| island, stair | near dark, cold spray, grey stone |
| lamp room | dark blue glass; then the lamp: warm gold and the beam over black sea |
| failure endings | full night, lighthouse dark (painted) |
