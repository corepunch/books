# Огонь на маяке

The example adventure for the authoring guide: a short journey in the manner of
*Zork: The Forces of Krill*, with seven places, strangers along the road, real
danger and seven endings. Every stage document exists, so it can be read beside
[authoring/README.md](../../authoring/README.md).

## Premise

Ten-year-old Varya must cross the bay to her sick grandfather's lighthouse and
light it before the storm brings the fishing boats — her father's among them —
home in the dark.

## High concept

A girl racing the tide and the dark across a whole bay: over tidal flats past
an old wreck, or along the cliffs over a rope bridge, then across a flooding
channel to a lighthouse on a rock.

Reader fantasy: doing the grown-up's job on the one night it matters.

## Journey line

- **Start:** grandfather Matvei's cottage in the fishing village.
- **Goal:** the lamp room at the top of the lighthouse on the island.
- **Opposing force:** the storm and the rising tide. The tide floods the flats,
  covers the stepping stones and closes routes as the story goes on; the storm
  arrives at the island.
- **Reason to hurry:** the sun is setting and the boats are still out.
- **Strangers:** Timka the fisherman's son (advice), a talking seal (a warning
  and a secret way), Agafya the shepherd (a warning about the rotten plank, a
  safer road and a word for the ferryman), Savely the ferryman (refuses, then
  relents or obeys Agafya).

## Audience

Ages 6–10. An adult reads aloud; an eight-year-old reads alone. Sentences of
ordinary length, concrete words, dialogue in «ёлочки». Peril is real but
nobody is hurt: failures end with Varya safe and the lighthouse dark.

## Tone

Windy, salty, brave. Wonder (a seal that speaks, the beam over the sea), danger
(a breaking plank, a wave over the stones) and warm grumpy adults.

## Hero

Varya, third person. Yellow oilskin coat, red knitted hat, one braid, dark
boots: she reads at any distance against grey sea and green cliffs.

## Setting, route and time

A northern fishing bay at sunset in a gathering storm, storybook-compressed so
the whole bay is about 70 m across. Route, in order:

1. The cottage (grandfather in bed, the dark lighthouse in the window).
2. The harbour and the fork by the old signpost.
3. Either **the tidal flats** (the wreck of the «Чайка», the seal's rock, the
   dry sandbar) or **the cliffs** (Agafya's hut and goats, the rope bridge over
   the gully, the sheep track). A path up the cliff face links the two.
4. The channel landing (the ferry hut, the jetty, the stepping stones).
5. The island and the lighthouse door.
6. The spiral stair and the lamp room.

Time runs from sunset to full dark; the light falls page by page.

## Acts

1. **Departure** (cottage, harbour, fork): the call and grandfather's warning;
   ends when Varya chooses a road.
2. **The road** (flats or cliffs, then the channel): strangers, the tide and
   the bridge; ends when she reaches the island.
3. **The lighthouse** (door, stair, lamp room): ends with the lamp lit or not.

## Size and budget

Seven locations, 34 pages (10 decision pages, 17 story pages, 7 endings) and
three invisible fact checks; 28 pictures, because consecutive pages of one
moment share a camera. Reading time 5–10 minutes per route.

## Structure

Branch and bottleneck. The road splits at the fork into the flats and the
cliffs (linked by the cliff-face path) and rejoins at the channel, which splits
again into the ferry and the stones and rejoins on the island.

## Goal and endings

| Ending | Kind | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| The lamp lit in time (`end-light`) | success | close the window first, as grandfather said |
| The lamp lit late (`end-late`) | partial | light the wick with the window open |
| Stayed with grandfather (`end-stay`) | failure, early and safe | do not set out |
| Stranded on the wreck (`end-wreck`) | failure | climb onto the deck as the tide comes |
| The rotten plank (`end-bridge`) | failure | step on the third plank after Agafya's warning |
| Swept off the stones (`end-stones`) | failure | jump for the far stone without the seal |
| Waited out the storm (`end-hut`) | failure | shelter in the ferry hut |

Failures offer «Попробовать снова» back to the last decision; the success
offers «Начать сначала». Sequel hook: an even older lighthouse on the Far
Island.

## Opening

The ordinary world is a sickroom: grandfather in bed, the storm at the window.
The strange event is the job passing to a child. The first decision comes on
page three.

## Contrast and secrets

| Act | Funny | Beautiful | Warm |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Timka's "long but dry" | the dark lighthouse like a snuffed candle | grandfather's hand |
| 2 | Timka's "Ну ты и капитан!"; Savely's "Вот бестолковая!" | the seal on the sandbar | Agafya vouching for Varya |
| 3 | — | the beam over the sea | the boats turning home |

Secret: following the seal opens the dry sandbar, and the seal returns to lead
her over the stones. Shortcuts: through the wreck (dangerous) and over the
bridge (dangerous). Kinder ways: the sheep track with Agafya, and the ferry.

## Exclusions

No inventory, nothing picked up or carried (the matches stay on the shelf in
the lamp room), no timers or dice, no typed commands, no sound.
