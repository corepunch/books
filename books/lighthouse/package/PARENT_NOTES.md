# Notes for the grown-up

Read these only if you are helping; they give the answers away gently.

## Hints, in order

**The fork.** Both roads can reach the lighthouse. — Timka said which is fast
and which is dry. — Either is fine; the dangers are different.

**The flats.** Who has spoken to Varya here? — The seal gave a warning about
the «Чайка». — Following the seal is the safe way, and it helps again later.

**The bridge.** What did Agafya say about the planks? — Look at the third
plank in the picture. — Step over it.

**The ferryman.** Why would Savely say yes? — Agafya mentioned him. — Walking
round with Agafya first earns her word; or come back soaked from the stones.

**The stones.** Is it safe to jump? — Who promised to help on the stones? —
Without the seal, turn back to the landing.

**The lamp.** What did grandfather say on the second page? — "First… then…" —
Close the window, then light the wick.

## Talk about it afterwards

- Why did Savely change his mind?
- Was it brave or foolish to leave grandfather? What would you have done?
- Which stranger would you trust first, and why?
