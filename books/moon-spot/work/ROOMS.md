# Room bible — Мира и лунный зайчик

One location, grandmother's kitchen, with two play areas: the floor and the
table. Coordinates are Scener centimetres, X east, Y north, Z up, origin at the
centre of the floor. **M** = modelled in the blockout, **P** = painted only.

## Grandmother's kitchen

### Resident story

Grandmother has cooked in this village kitchen for forty years. Tonight she
drank her tea at the table by the window, unpinned her hair in front of her
round hand mirror, banked the stove for the night and went to bed in the next
room, leaving the mirror where it stood. Бублик has slept on the mat in front
of the stove every winter night of his long life; Mira's basket stands in the
quiet corner by the dresser because that is where grandmother put it when
Mira was small.

### Walk-in description

The kitchen is dark and warm. The first thing you see is a round, silvery patch
of light on the lower door of the dresser, too bright for a moon so low. The
floorboards are wide, worn pale along the path from the door to the stove,
and cold near the window. The stove ticks as it cools; through the little
grille in its fire door the embers glow red, and a warm smell of birch smoke
and dog hangs over the mat where Бублик snores. Over the table the window
holds a big low moon; the small top pane is open, and every breath of night air
lifts the edge of the curtain.

### Routes as physical moves (M)

`floor 0 → stool seat 45 (jump, 45 up) → table top 75 (30 up, 6 cm gap, front
paws first)`, and the same in reverse. Mira's limit is 50 cm up (CHARACTERS),
so both hops fit. For the curtain joke she hops from the table (75) onto the
sill board (100, 25 up) at its west end and reaches the west panel's edge.

### Atmosphere (P)

The tick of the cooling stove; Бублик's snore; warm birch smoke and dog on one
side of the room, cold window air and tea on the other; dust turning slowly in
the moonbeam.

### Sightlines and connections

- From the floor: the table, stool and window are always visible behind; the
  mirror is a small bright point on the table.
- The table pages look back at the table from high in the south, so the
  dresser (left), the window (centre) and Бублик's mat (lower right) share one
  frame with the mirror. The beam runs
  from the mirror across the room to wherever the spot lies.
- The door to the hall stays closed; grandmother is asleep behind it.

### Quiet surfaces for text

- The plaster wall above the dresser and to the left of the window (upper left
  in floor wides).
- The dark window glass and the wall above the curtain rod (table cameras).
- The dark floor in front of the door (south) for low cameras.
- The ceiling and upper wall above the stove for the endings.

### Detail hierarchy

1. Mira, the moon-spot, Бублик's face.
2. The mirror, the stool and the table edge, the dresser door.
3. The stove glow, the window, the basket.
4. Crockery, curtains, photograph — present but quiet, never as bright as the
   spot.
