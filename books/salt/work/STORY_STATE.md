# Story state

Facts only ever turn on. Retry restores the snapshot taken when that decision
was entered, so a failed reading does not spend a fact and does not un-see a
clip. Nothing queues, and nothing moves on its own.

The best ending still needs only SEEN, GLIMPSED, and HEARD. BOARD, PENRY, and
PEG change what the road says. MET and LEFT only keep a clip and a man from
repeating. NAMED and RAIL only keep a question from being asked twice.

Progress the reader can name: hired, the margin, the lamp, the face, the
warning, the hour, the dry room. The board, Penry, and the peg are the road.
Reading the hour to the fog, the sluice, the lamp, the glass, or the harbor
does not set a fact. Trying again restores the decision, the book, and the
facts as they were when that screen was entered.

No unwinnable pocket. The ledger stays in the coat. The dry room's door goes
down. The alley and the sluice walk both ways. A screen that will not hear
the hour hides its circles while the book is open, and brings them back when
the book is shut.
