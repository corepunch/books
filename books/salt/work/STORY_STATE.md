# Story state

Facts only ever turn on. Retry restores the snapshot taken when that decision was entered, so a failed reading does not spend a fact. Nothing queues, and nothing moves on its own. Two facts cannot fight, because no page clears one.

| Flag | Meaning | Set when | Read by |
|---|---|---|---|
| READ | The margin says: to her, in this room, not the water, not the rain | The folder page | The office door, which stays shut until then |
| SEEN | Thomas stood under the lamp and was gone | The window clip | The best ending |
| GLIMPSED | A face turned under the pilings | The first look at the water | The best ending, and the ledger's line on the wharf |
| HEARD | Marsh's warning | The first talk on the deck | The best ending |
| KNEW | The ledger showed the name, the hour, and her line | Taking the ledger | The floor's second failure text. Carrying the book implies this, because the take is the page that sets it. |

| Thing | Bit | Starts | Moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ledger | LEDGER | Lying in the shack | Taken by the ledger choice. Carried after that. Never removed. Holding it, on a decision, is how a reading is offered. |

Checks, and the pair they guard:

| Check | Pass | Otherwise | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| dock-back | Carrying the ledger → wharf with the book | Wharf without it | One return path from water, Marsh, and the shack door |
| marsh-route | Carrying → Marsh refuses the book | marsh-heard | He behaves differently once the book is in the coat |
| marsh-heard | HEARD → the short repeat | The first warning | The warning is not a second clue dump |
| water-seen | GLIMPSED → the harbor keeps its secret | The first turning face | Looking twice does not set the fact again in the prose |
| leave-check | Carrying → climb to the office | The dull walk home | Empty hands are an ending. The book is the hill. |
| shack-check | Carrying → the bare shack | The shack with the book on the table | The ledger choice exists only while it lies there |
| best-check | SEEN and GLIMPSED and HEARD → the full ending | The quiet ending | Optional discoveries change the last page, not the win |

Progress the reader can name: hired, the margin, the lamp, the face, the warning, the hour, the dry room. Score is the ending, not a number.

No unwinnable pocket. The ledger stays in the coat. The dry room's door goes down. A failure retries the decision where the book and the facts were still yours.
