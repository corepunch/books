# Objects

The engine has no parser. A thing the reader can touch is either a choice anchor or the one carried object. Nouns in the prose that are not anchors are the picture: the lamp, the telephone, the Patience, the pilings. They are not a second ledger or a second door.

| Thing | Where it starts | What the reader does | Roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Folder | Office desk, anchor `folder` | Read once. Later, the place the hour is spoken. | Clue, goal, Edith's chair in the room |
| Window | Office, anchor `pane` | Look. Later, a wrong mouth for the hour. | Clue, risk, the man under the lamp |
| Office door | Anchor `door` | Opens onto the hill after the folder. From the dry room, goes back to the wharf. | Route |
| Harbor water | Wharf, anchor `water` | Look down. While the ledger is held, a wrong reading. | Clue, risk, the changed face |
| Caleb Marsh | Patience, anchor `mate` | Talk. With the book in hand he will not let it be read on the deck. | Clue, warning, dry joke |
| Shack door | Anchor `shack-door` | Enter. | Route |
| Lane | Anchor `lane` | Empty hands: go home. Book shut: climb to the office. Book held open: read to the fog. | Route, risk, the partial ending |
| Ledger | On the shack table, anchor `book`, id `ledger` | Take. Hold to read the hour where you stand. | Clue, tool, the summons |
| Trap | Shack floor, anchor `trap` | Open. Before or after the hour, he rises. | Risk, the failure under the floor |
| Shack door out | Anchor `out` | Back to the dock. | Route |

The ledger is the only portable object. Its icon is `rooms/item-ledger.jpg`. Nothing else is carried. The folder, the doors, the trap, and the boat stay where they are.

Holding the ledger replaces the room circles with the places that hour can be spoken. Putting it away brings the doors back. A page with no reading-circle means this is the wrong room for the hour, which the line under his name already said.
