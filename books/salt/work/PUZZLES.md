# Puzzles

The case is one puzzle in three layers. The hour is a summons. The only fair place to speak it is the dry room, onto Edith's folder. Everything else is a mouth he can answer.

## Speak the hour in the dry room

- Goal: ending-best or ending-quiet.
- Gate: READ (her pencil), the ledger carried, KNEW (the hour has been seen in the book). The best ending also wants SEEN, GLIMPSED, and HEARD. Those three are not a lock. Skipping them still wins; it wins more quietly.
- Clues, in the order a reader meets them:
  1. Edith, in the chair: read the hour to her in this room, and leave the floor shut.
  2. The folder margin: to her, in this room. Not to the water. Not in the rain.
  3. Marsh: the book is on the table, the floor answers, the rain is not a room, carry it up the hill.
  4. The ledger: his name, an hour already passed, and her line under it. Say it where the floor is dry.
- Solution: take the ledger, keep it shut on the lane, and at the office hold it and choose the folder.
- Softlock: none. The door in the dry room returns to the wharf. The folder, if the book is shut, says to open it. Retry on a failure restores the decision, the facts, and the book.

## Wrong attempts

Each is a choice the picture and the text already named.

| Attempt | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Open the trap before reading the hour | Shack | The face from the folder rises. He did not need the hour. |
| Open the trap after reading it | Shack | The face rises because the name was brought down to the water. |
| Read the hour to the harbor | Wharf, ledger held, the water | The face turns up and stays. |
| Read the hour to the lane | Wharf, ledger held, the lane | The fog keeps your pace. Partial: you are alive, and followed. |
| Read the hour to the glass | Office, ledger held, the window | The latch rises from outside. |
| Leave the dock with empty hands | Wharf, the lane | A dull morning. The folder is still on the desk. |
| Read it on Marsh's deck | Wharf, the mate, book in hand | He puts a palm between the book and the boat and sends you up the hill. |
| Look at the water a second time | Wharf | The harbor keeps what it turned. The name is in the book. |
| Choose the folder with the book shut | Office, returned | The page tells you to open the ledger. The case does not end. |

Hint if someone is stuck on the dock with the book: her pencil said where. The lane with the book shut is the hill. Holding it open spends the hour on whatever you touch.

There is no second key and no timed clock. The tide has already passed; the hour does not expire while the reader thinks.
