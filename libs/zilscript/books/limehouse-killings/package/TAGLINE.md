# The Limehouse Killings - Tagline

## Primary Tagline
**"In fog-shrouded London, a detective's reputation hangs on one question: Who killed Lord Ashworth?"**

## Alternative Taglines

### Atmospheric
- "The gas lamps flicker. The fog rolls in. The killer waits."
- "In the shadows of Limehouse, a nobleman lies dead."

### Mystery
- "The door was locked from the inside. The killer must be inside the manor."
- "Three suspects. Five clues. One truth."

### Action
- "Gather evidence. Interrogate suspects. Name the killer."
- "The game is afoot. The clock is ticking."

### Intellectual
- "Can you solve the puzzle before the killer strikes again?"
- "Every clue tells a story. Every suspect hides a secret."

## Tagline Usage

### Title Screen

"Step into the fog of Victorian London. A nobleman lies dead in his locked study. Three suspects. Five clues. One truth. Can you solve the murder of Lord Ashworth?"

### Box Art
"The Limehouse Killings: A Victorian London Murder Mystery"

### Social Media
"New text adventure: The Limehouse Killings. Can you catch the killer? #InteractiveFiction #MysteryGame #VictorianLondon"

## Tagline Tone Guide
- Mysterious, not scary
- Intellectual, not action-packed
- Atmospheric, not gory
- Engaging, not passive
