# The Limehouse Killings - Synopsis

## Short Synopsis (50 words)
Lord Ashworth lies dead in his locked study. You are the private detective hired to solve the murder. In fog-shrouded Victorian London, gather evidence, interrogate three suspects, and name the killer before they strike again. Can you solve the mystery and secure your reputation?

## Medium Synopsis (100 words)
The year is 1888. Lord Ashworth, a wealthy industrialist, has been found dead in his locked study at Ashworth Manor in the Limehouse district. Scotland Yard is baffled. Lady Ashworth has hired you to investigate before the press gets wind of the scandal.

As a private detective in Victorian London, you must explore the manor, gather evidence, and interrogate three suspects: the nervous butler Mr. Hudson, the cold Lady Ashworth, and the arrogant Dr. Moriarty. Five key pieces of evidence point to the killer, but can you connect the clues before they escape?

## Full Synopsis (200 words)
In the gaslit streets of Victorian London, a nobleman lies dead. Lord Ashworth, wealthy industrialist and patron of the arts, has been found in his locked study, victim of a violent murder. Scotland Yard is baffled—the room was sealed from the inside, yet the victim bears the marks of a killer's rage.

Lady Ashworth, the victim's wife, has hired you—a private detective of some renown—to investigate before the press gets wind of the scandal. Your reputation hangs on solving this case.

As you explore Ashworth Manor, you discover a web of secrets: a nervous butler with a key to the study, a cold widow with an alibi that doesn't quite hold, and an arrogant scientist with access to rare poisons. Five key pieces of evidence lie hidden in the manor: an unsent letter, a blood-stained knife, a locked box, a poison bottle, and a secret ledger.

Can you gather the evidence, connect the clues, and name the killer? Or will the murderer escape, leaving you disgraced and Lord Ashworth's death unpunished?
