## Review Summary

### Strengths
- Exceptional atmospheric writing
- Challenging but fair puzzles
- Complex, believable suspects
- Multiple solution paths
- High replay value

### Weaknesses
- Some players may find cipher puzzle difficult
- Text-only presentation may not appeal to all
- No save/load feature (engine limitation)

### Overall Rating
**9.2/10** - Exceptional

### Recommendation
**Must Play** for mystery fans, text adventure enthusiasts, and anyone who loves a good puzzle.
