# The Last Toymaker's Apprentice

**Genre:** Text adventure (interactive fiction)  
**Play time:** 60-90 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Light-to-medium (all ages 10+)

## Tagline

*One night to save the toys. One fox to learn to trust. One heart to wind before dawn.*

## Synopsis

You are Pip, a tiny workshop gnome apprentice no taller than a teacup, working for Grandfather Tolliver, the last toymaker in Wrenfold. Every night, the toys wake up. Tonight, they may never wake again.

The workshop key — the source of all magic in town — is missing from its hook. Pip must venture beyond the cosy workshop into the snowy streets of Wrenfold, where every streetlamp, mailbox, and shopfront was once a discarded toy. The key was taken by Nutmeg, a fox-shaped toy so long alone she no longer believes she was ever loved.

To save Wrenfold, Pip must befriend the fox, unravel the toymaker's disappearance, and rewind the workshop's clockwork heart before dawn — with help from a pompous nutcracker, a riddling cuckoo clock, and a rag doll who sings in secrets.

**The Last Toymaker's Apprentice** is a warm, bittersweet tale of kindness, loneliness, and what it means to fix the things that are broken — including ourselves.

## Key Features

- **Three-act structure** with escalating stakes, from cosy workshop to snow-covered town to clockwork heart
- **Four memorable NPCs** — each with distinct personality, dialogue trees, and story arcs
- **Emotional puzzle design** — puzzles ask for compassion, patience, and listening, not just inventory chess
- **Branching ending** — the finale evaluates how you treated every character
- **Progressive hint system** — layered hints from Old Tick's riddles keep players moving
- **Tick-clock urgency** — 200 turns until dawn, visible through atmospheric warnings
