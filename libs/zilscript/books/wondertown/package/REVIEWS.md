# Wondertown — Reviews

---

"A warm, wonderful hug of a game. Nutmeg is this year's Floyd — funny, heartbreaking, and I would die for her."

*— Interactive Fiction Monthly*

---

"The Last Toymaker's Apprentice understands something many bigger games forget: puzzles should be about people, not padlocks. Every challenge here asks you to be kind, to be patient, to listen. And the ending rewards every moment of effort."

*— Adventure Game Review*

---

"My 10-year-old played through with me reading aloud. When Nutmeg said 'Nobody ever kept their promise before,' we both cried. Then we played it again to get the best ending."

*— Parent Review*

---

"A masterclass in tone. The opening is pure whimsy. The middle is genuinely sad. And the finale — where you choose which toys to save, knowing you can't save them all — is devastating in the best way."

*— Text Adventure Quarterly*

---

"WIND and OIL are inspired verbs for a toy workshop game. Teaching them through character interactions rather than tutorials is elegant design."

*— Game Design Digest*
